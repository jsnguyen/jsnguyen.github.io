About the models

Original follows Keck's NIRC2 efficiency calculator, written before the November 2023 Archon upgrade. Dither moves take 6 s.

Updated is fit to frame headers taken after the upgrade: 14,311 of our frames, checked against 139,541 in the Keck Observatory Archive.

  • Readout: one 200 kHz read per coadd for CDS, N for MCDS with N read pairs.
  • Transfer: 52 ns per pixel per read pair, after each coadd.
  • Between frames: 4.0 s plus 0.28 s per megapixel. Some scripts add about 5 s.
  • Dither moves: 25 s typical or 9 s fast, with D + 1 moves and none for a single position, as in section 2.4 of the observer's manual.

Neither model adds the old 15 s per LGS dither, since it no longer applies. Legal subarray sizes follow Keck's subarray table.
