Hi! I'm Jayke Samson Nguyen

5th Year PhD Candidate @ UCSD A&A Department

IPAC Visiting Graduate Student Fellow


I'm an astrophysicist working on high-contrast imaging with adaptive optics. My main science focus is finding and characterizing exoplanets and protoplanetary disks through ground-based direct imaging.


I work on the full-stack of astronomical instrumentation, from building new instruments to developing novel post-processing techniques to analyze the data. I am also a part of the Gemini Planet Imager 2.0 team.

Research

Direct Imaging of Exoplanets in the Mid-IR

I'm looking for exoplanets in the mid-IR (>4.7 µm). Planets at these wavelengths are older and colder than the current population of planets we've directly imaged.

Keck Adaptive Optics Image Sharpening

I am currently working on updating the image sharpening code for the Keck adaptive optics benches with newer gradient-based phase retrieval algorithms.

The Pyramid Wavefront Sensor Upgrade of GPI 2.0

I helped build the new pyramid wavefront sensor going into the Gemini Planet Imager 2.0. I worked on integrating the movable mirrors, needed to direct the beam through the wavefront sensor.

GPI 2.0 AO Simulations

I've developed AO simulations of the new AO system on GPI 2.0 using HCIpy. The simulations were designed to test performance and efficacy of the upgrades to the system.
