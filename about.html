--- title: About ---
Hello! I am Jayke Nguyen and I graduated from UC Berkeley in 2018 with two bachelors degrees in physics and astrophysics. I currently work at Livermore National Laboratory developing adaptive optics systems and measuring the parallax of nearby brown dwarfs. In Fall 2021 I will be pursuing a PhD in Physics at UCSD.

In terms of scientific interests, I am eager to learn more about exoplanets and instrumentation used to find exoplanets. Specifically, I hope to learn more about telescope and optical design that helps enable direct imaging of exoplanets and protoplanetary systems. Furthermore, I hope to learn more about the hydrodynamic aspects of protoplanetary disks and the various phenomena that allow for the formation of stable systems of planetary bodies around young stars.

I really enjoy coding and am lucky to be able to be a scientific programmer as my day job. I hope to become a more experienced programmer and learn more languages such as Fortran 77/95, Haskell, and Julia.
